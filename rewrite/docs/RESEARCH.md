# Competitor review — Ummy Video Downloader (11 November 2026)

Windows listing: https://ummy-video-downloader.ru.uptodown.com/windows — published as Ummy, version 1.16.13.0 (2024), rating 2.6/5 across 14 reviews.
Android listing: https://ummy-video-downloader.ru.uptodown.com/android — published as Nyit Nyot Ah, version 4.0.0 (2019), rating 4.4/5 across 5 reviews on the Russian page.

Crucial: these listings identify DIFFERENT DEVELOPERS. Shared names do not establish a common codebase or a first-party Android version.

## Positive evidence
- Paste-link workflow and quality dropdown are simple and discoverable; some users report ease of use and good speed.
- Windows UX includes preview, MP4/MP3 output, older-version playlist selection, save folder; Softpedia review finds it easy to use.
- https://biblsoft.ru/windows/internet/downloaders/415-ummy-video-downloader
- https://www.softpedia.com/get/Internet/Download-Managers/Ummy-Video-Downloader.shtml

## Negative evidence
- Windows reviewers report paste or downloads failing; unexpected paid limits; payment/renewal frustrations.
- Trustpilot lists 9 reviews and a 2.2/5 score; some allege lifetime-licence failures, inability to cancel or contact support and downloads failing. These are unverified users' statements, not independently confirmed misconduct.
- Uptodown editorial team reports the Android version's download arrow disappearing, making its tested download workflow unusable.
- https://www.trustpilot.com/review/videodownloader.ummy.net

## Product lessons
1. Fewest possible steps: URL → metadata → true available formats → download.
2. Never claim a link or format works until tested.
3. Verify a complete nonempty output before showing success.
4. Clear error messages, retries, pause/resume, crash-safe state.
5. No concealed restrictions, misleading subscription prompts or mandatory login for public links.
6. Clearly distinguish desktop/mobile features and build automated + physical-device tests.

Limits: Reviews are self-selected, often old and have small samples. We did not install or reverse-engineer Ummy itself. Security reports about unofficial downloads do not prove anything about the vendor's official builds.
